# The Super-Smart, Unbreakable Piggy Bank

**Audience:** Children, teens, and anyone new to finance  
**Version:** 6.Omnibus_Adelic  
**Reading level:** Ages 8+

> **Languages:** English · [Eesti](et/04-children-beginners.md) · [Русский](ru/04-children-beginners.md)

## Meet your money robot

Imagine a **robot piggy bank** that is also a tiny mathematician. You put money in, and the robot figures out:

- Where your money can grow the fastest  
- How to move it without spilling any (no wasted fees)  
- How to share some profits with people who need help  
- How to keep a diary that nobody can sneak in and erase  

That robot is the **Utah Finance Library**.

## Why normal banks feel like leaky buckets

When you move coins between piggy banks at different places, a little bit often **falls out**:

- Someone charges you to carry the coins  
- Money sits in a slow account while a faster one waits  
- Paperwork takes days  

Our robot tries to **plug the leaks** so more coins stay working for you.

## The sunflower secret

Picture many friends picking toys from a table. If everyone grabs at once, elbows bump!

The robot uses a **sunflower pattern** in math: it sorts money into small groups where friends never bump. Each group is called a **petal**. Petals move smoothly without traffic jams.

That is the **k-sunflower** idea—fancy name, simple idea: **organize so nothing crashes**.

## The wave brain (PINN)

Old guessing games memorize yesterday’s weather and assume tomorrow is the same. Markets change!

Our robot’s brain uses **physics waves**—like ripples in a pond—to make steadier guesses. It also has a rule that says: **“Don’t get too wild when things get scary.”** That helps on stormy market days.

## The shape detective (market geometry)

Imagine the stock market is a giant trampoline. When things are calm, the
trampoline is smooth. When something scary happens, it gets **bumpy and curvy**.

Our robot is a **shape detective**. Instead of guessing tomorrow's weather from
an old photo, it **feels the bumps right now** by watching prices wiggle. From
how curvy the trampoline is, it figures out:

- **"How big could a fall be?"** — like knowing how deep a dip in the trampoline
  goes before you jump.  
- **"Is everything clumping together?"** — when all the toys roll into one pile,
  that is a warning that a storm might be coming.

The detective also has a magic eraser called **Ricci flow** that smooths away
tiny meaningless jiggles, so it only pays attention to the **real** bumps. Cool,
right? It is like cleaning a foggy window so you can see clearly.

## Instant trade fairy (Adelic bypass)

Usually when two people trade something big, a **referee** holds treasure from both sides until everyone promises the trade is fair. The treasure just **sits there**, doing nothing.

Our robot checks both piggy banks with math tricks. If both truly have enough, the trade can finish **right away**—no referee treasure pile needed. That frees money to keep growing.

## Sharing rules (the nice parts)

Every time the robot makes extra money for you, it slices the pie:

| Slice | How much | Why |
|-------|----------|-----|
| Builder slice | 2.3 pennies per dollar earned (2.3%) | Thanks Utah Hans for building the robot |
| Help-the-world slice | 5.7 pennies per dollar (5.7%) | Food, shelter, and help for people in need |
| Your slice | The rest | Stays in your piggy bank to grow more |

You still get most of the pie—and you help the world **automatically**.

## Safe locks (vault)

Big moves need **two or more keys** to turn at once—like a treasure chest that needs both parents’ keys. The robot writes every move in a **diary with tamper-proof seals** (cryptographic hashes).

## Try it (with a grown-up)

Grown-ups can run the demo on a computer:

```bash
python -m src.app.utah_prime_sieve_daemon
```

You might see messages about “capital leakage” (leaky bucket found) and “petals” (sunflower groups). That means the robot is working!

## Remember

- Money robots are **tools**, not magic fortune tellers  
- Real life still needs honest adults, rules, and kindness  
- Sharing a little on every gain makes you a **money hero**  

Have fun learning—and always ask questions when a word sounds too grown-up!
